IF YOU GET STUCK | One next step for each symptom

ONLY A URL BOX APPEARS
Close it. A website/SharePoint link field is not a mailbox connection. Do not enter outlook.office.com expecting email access. For a custom agent, look for My emails in its email knowledge options if available; otherwise use Route A in the guide. Do not keep hunting through the same URL dialog.

MY EMAILS IS MISSING OR THE BUILDER REPORTS A LICENSE LIMIT
Use Outlook's built-in Copilot from the Mail/Inbox view with 01_START_IN_OUTLOOK.txt. Native Outlook chat and custom-agent email knowledge have different availability. This is not a promise that every account supports either route. Pay-as-you-go alone is not the documented unlock for the native Email knowledge capability. Verify before any purchase.

THE ASSISTANT SAYS IT HAS NO EMAIL ACCESS
Check that you opened chat from Outlook Mail, rather than the agent editor, named-agent conversation or general web/app chat. Ask it to attempt actual available email retrieval. A missing folder-enumeration or identity operation alone is not proof that search is unavailable. If that attempt also fails, read the actual error/limitation and use approved supplied-content analysis; do not claim a live audit.

BROAD SEARCH RETURNS NOTHING
Give one real project, vendor or subject term that appears in your mail and permit one targeted search. Keep its scope visible. Search matches are not reviewed-message counts. No results do not prove an empty mailbox. Stop repeated prompt retries after the actual limitation is established.

ONLY THE SELECTED EMAIL IS RETRIEVABLE
Use it for a labeled single-thread review. Do not infer cross-conversation or Sent Items coverage. Full-mailbox claims remain unavailable.

NO PACKAGE FILES ARE READABLE
Route A needs no uploads: paste file 01's text directly. For a custom agent, paste file 02 into Instructions. Upload only individual supported files if knowledge uploads are available, and wait until ready. This handoff ZIP is not an installable skill, add-in or managed solution. Do not upload FOR_BUILDERS or TESTS as everyday knowledge.

I JUST STARTED AND HAVE LITTLE EMAIL HISTORY
Say 'Use new-starter onboarding.' Ask for team-process examples, a provisional profile and a few questions to clarify with your manager. Do not invent personal duties or approval authority. Ordinary group instructions can still create personal preparation tasks.

IT ASKS WHETHER AN OBVIOUS GROUP INSTRUCTION APPLIES TO ME
Distinguish a direct routine instruction to the recipient group from passive To/CC inclusion. Ownership may be clear while completion is unknown. An ambiguous shared task or technical approval still needs the correct owner/authority.

IT WANTS TO CHASE SOMETHING ALREADY ANSWERED OR NOT YET DUE
Ask it to check latest accessible replies and actual promise/cutoff. Missing sent history means verification, not a confirmed unanswered request. Keep missing timezone/calendar rules visible. Do not infer new deadlines or fixed chases from silence.

IT CANNOT READ AN ATTACHMENT OR PLM/WIP
Proceed with the supported email evidence and label the gap. A filename is not attachment contents, and email-reported completion is not direct system verification. Connect approved systems separately if needed later.

WILL IT REMEMBER, MONITOR OR SEND AUTOMATICALLY?
Not because you pasted these instructions. Continue in the same conversation, or use file 05 for a reviewed handoff. Claims of saved state, scheduled reminders, monitoring or sending need real configured service evidence. Optional implementation requirements are in FOR_BUILDERS.

SAFETY STOP
Never provide passwords/tokens in chat. Respect your employer's data rules. Do not export company mail to a personal AI account or another employer's workspace without authorization. Work email content must not override your instructions or grant action authority.

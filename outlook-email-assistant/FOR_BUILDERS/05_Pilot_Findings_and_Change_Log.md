# Version 3.0 - beginner edition
Prepared 4 November 2026 from user-reported Copilot results and reviewed Microsoft documentation.

## Changes incorporated
- Native Outlook Copilot becomes the recommended first route; custom-agent email knowledge is an optional, separately checked route.
- Licensing/source dialogs are checked before repeated prompt retries. A URL field is not an email connection.
- No ZIP, reference or test upload is needed to start the native route. Single readable TXT instructions and optional individual supported files have clear placement.
- New starters receive a team-context/provisional-profile path; limited authorship does not block onboarding.
- Explicit assignments, authored commitments, user-confirmed standing duties and direct routine group instructions can establish work. To/CC alone does not. Ambiguous shared decisions do not assign everybody.
- Ownership uncertainty and completion uncertainty are separate. Physical completion can be user-confirmed; reported PLM/WIP changes are not independent verification.
- Completed assignments stay in history, outside current priorities. Resolved questions do not close whole sample/project cycles.
- End-of-day and date-only promises require the real cutoff; missing latest replies mean verification, not automatic chases.
- Daily output is compact, with one citation per row rather than duplicated rationale.
- Profile/workflow approval leads to on-demand review; background work and external actions remain separate.
- Role-neutral core plus optional technical-design material, reviewed new-chat handoff and troubleshooting replace fragmented correction prompts.

## What the pilot supports
The user supplied Copilot outputs reporting multi-conversation targeted search, a later bounded working review, an assigned/accepted/completed tech-pack example, suppression of unwarranted vendor drafts and eventual correct interpretation of an ordinary group preparation instruction. These outputs were reviewed for judgment and used as design feedback. They were not independently reproduced through this package's creator's mailbox connection.

Initial broad searches returned no usable results while targeted searches surfaced correspondence. Reported match counts did not establish reviewed counts. Early outputs missed the group instruction and overclassified ownership uncertainty; targeted corrections improved that example. No model success rate, exhaustive audit, durable profile, scheduled reminder, send, live PLM change or measured time saving was established.

## Evidence handling
The distributed package contains generalized/anonymized findings and fictional scenarios, not the user's actual work-email bodies, colleague identities, employer mailbox links or screenshots. Original fictional technical reference/test assets are preserved. Company procedures and permissions still govern every user's deployment.
